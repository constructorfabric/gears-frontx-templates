What you asked for

You asked for one page, no options, for your name, your language and your emails; the email address itself is assumed to come from sign-in.

"Add an account settings page where I can change my display name, my language and which emails I get. One page is enough, no options needed."

What we assumed

  • The person changes only their own account.
  • The email address comes from sign-in and is shown, not changed here.
  • Security alerts cannot be turned off.
  • Changes apply when saved, not on every click.

Not part of this page

  • Password, sign-in methods and deleting the account.
  • Settings of other people or of the whole team.

How it works

The phone shows the two cards one under the other; the desktop sets them side by side with Save under them.

On a phone

  1. Cards in one column Profile first, then the emails; the two cards no longer sit side by side.
  2. Save at the end Below the email switches, so a person scrolls down to save.

On a desktop

  1. Display name The name other people see; it cannot be empty.
  2. Email Shown for reference; changing it is open
  3. Language Changes the language of every page after saving. accepted
  4. Email switches One per kind of email; security alerts stay on. accepted
  5. Save changes Saves everything at once and says so.
  6. Last saved When the settings last changed.

Reads: exists today the name and email of the person, from AccountsApiService; needs a new data request the language, the email choices and saving them.

Addresses

The page has one address, and each person who opens it sees their own settings.

The app's links carry the screen in the address (/?screen=account), not the registered route /account, so a shared link has that form. The address holds no account, so a shared link opens the reader's own settings; Back returns to the page the reader came from.

Built from what the app already has

The cards, fields, select, switches and button the Home page already uses; nothing new.

Data it needs

Your name and email exist today; the language, the email choices and saving them need a new data request.

  • Display name and email: who the person is. exists today AccountsApiService, the current user
  • Language and email choices: what the person picked. needs a new data request to be agreed
  • Saving: writes the name, the language and the email choices. needs a new data request to be agreed

The drawings show sample data.

States

Only the full page is drawn; loading, saved and could not save are one line each.

  • Full: the drawing above. drawn
  • Loading: grey lines in each card until the settings arrive. not drawn
  • Saved: a short message that the changes are saved. not drawn
  • Could not save: says so above the button, keeps what was typed and offers Try again. not drawn
